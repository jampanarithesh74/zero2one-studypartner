# 🎓 ZERO2ONE

> **Your Academic Companion**
>
> **Smart Tools. Structured Resources. A Better You.**

ZERO2ONE is a student-centric academic platform designed to simplify the engineering journey through structured resources, smart calculators, productivity tools, and personalized academic experiences.

Built **for students, by students** ❤️.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔐 Smart Login System

* College email authentication
* Personalized dashboard
* Automatic academic space allocation

---

## 📚 Academic Modes

ZERO2ONE supports multiple academic journeys:

### 📖 Regular Student

For students pursuing their degree in a regular flow.

### 🔄 Rejoinee Student

For students continuing after an academic gap.

### 📋 Supply Student

For students clearing backlogs and getting back on track.

---

## 🏠 Dashboard

A clean and minimal dashboard containing:

* Welcome section
* Academic mode information
* Semester progress tracker
* Quick actions
* Upcoming examinations
* Notifications

## 📚 Resources

Structured resources organized as:

```
Year
 └── Department
      └── Semester
           └── Subject
                ├── Notes
                ├── PYQs
                ├── Syllabus
                └── PDFs
```

Supported departments:

* Information Technology
* Computer Science Engineering
* Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
* Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
* Electronics and Communication Engineering
* Electrical and Electronics Engineering
* Mechanical Engineering
* Civil Engineering
* And more...

# 🛠 Academic Tools

## 🧮 Academic Calculators

### SGPA Predictor

Predict semester GPA based on expected marks.

Features:

* Mid-1 marks
* Mid-2 marks
* Assignment marks
* Semester examination marks
* Lab evaluation support
* Automatic grade calculation
* Instant SGPA prediction

---

### CGPA Calculator

Calculate cumulative GPA across semesters.

Features:

* Semester-wise GPA entry
* Dynamic semester slots
* Partial calculation support
* Sequential validation
* Instant CGPA computation
